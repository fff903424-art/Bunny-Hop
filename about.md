# Bunny Hop

Automatically make your player jump at a fixed interval while playing Geometry Dash.

## What it does

Bunny Hop periodically applies an upward velocity to the active player during gameplay.

## Settings

- **Bunny Hop** — Enable or disable the mod.
- **Jump Interval** — Choose how frequently the automatic jump occurs, from `0.1` to `10.0` seconds.

The default interval is **0.5 seconds**.

## Compatibility

- Geometry Dash 2.2081
- Geode 5.10.1
- Android64

This is the initial version of Bunny Hop.
